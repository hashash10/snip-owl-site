PRIVACY POLICY

Your captures belong to you.

This policy explains what Snip Owl handles when you use the Chrome extension and cloud library, why it is needed, and the choices you have.

Last updated September 13, 2026

What Snip Owl stores

When you capture a screenshot or short tab recording, Snip Owl stores the media and related details such as the page title, source URL, capture time, dimensions, duration, optional notes, and whether tab audio was included. The extension keeps a local copy in your Chrome profile.

If you sign in and enable cloud use, Snip Owl also stores your Google-provided account identifier and email address, plus private copies of your captures and their metadata in Supabase.

How the information is used

Snip Owl uses this information to save, organize, search, sync, display, play, and delete your own captures. Account data connects the extension and web library to the same private workspace. Snip Owl does not sell personal information or use capture contents for advertising.

Browser permissions

The extension uses Chrome permissions to capture the tab you explicitly choose, show its controls, save media locally, and communicate with the private cloud service. Tab audio is captured only when you select that option. Snip Owl does not record continuously in the background.

Service providers

Google provides account sign-in. Supabase provides authentication, database, private object storage, and server functions. These providers process data as needed to deliver their services and apply their own security and privacy terms.

Google API data

Snip Owl’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Access and sharing

Cloud records are protected by account-level access rules. Media is kept in private storage and opened through short-lived links. The current product does not create public links to your captures. If public sharing is added later, it will require an action you choose.

Retention and deletion

Local captures remain in the Chrome profile until you delete them or remove the related browser data. Cloud captures remain until you delete them from your library. Deleting a cloud capture removes its database record and stored media through the application’s deletion flow.

Security

Snip Owl uses encrypted network connections, private storage, authenticated requests, and account-level database policies. No online system can promise absolute security, but access is limited to what the product needs to operate.

Children

Snip Owl is not directed to children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Changes to this policy

This page will be updated when the product’s data practices materially change. The date above shows the latest revision.
